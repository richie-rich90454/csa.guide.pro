## Welcome to the CSA Guide

Welcome to Top Tips for AP Computer Science A!

In this website, we will guide you, through a series of examples, to write trash code, or turn your current code to trash. We now cover **both** the classic ten-unit AP CS A curriculum and the new four-unit curriculum that launched in the 2025-26 school year. Pick your poison below; both paths teach the same Java, just chopped up differently by the folks at College Board.

Hopefully, you already know how to write the following:

```java
public class Main{
    public static void main(String[] args){
         System.out.println("Hello World");
    }
}
```

### Choose your curriculum

College Board reorganized AP CS A from ten units into four starting with the May 2026 exam. The content is mostly the same - just rearranged, with inheritance removed and text files/data sets added. We support **both** in full detail.
